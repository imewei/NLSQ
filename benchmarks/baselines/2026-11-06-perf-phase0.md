arm = same fit with `select_step` wrapped and every device-to-host conversion (`__float__`, `__int__`, `__bool__`,
`__index__`, `__array__`, `item`, `tolist` of a JAX array) timed as "wait" (`block_until_ready` of the not-yet-ready value)
